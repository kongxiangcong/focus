# Frozen assessment rubric

Freeze the round before the first answer: covered nodes/claims, ordered questions, source anchors, expected points, critical errors, pass rules, and remediation rules. Keep it immutable after answering begins.

Ask one question at a time. Preserve every answer verbatim before interpretation. Clarify wording without revealing solution paths. Diagnose only after the required round is complete, and report demonstrated/missing points plus evidence references for every node.

Use `no-evidence`, `misconception`, or `partial` to produce `needs-remediation`. Use a closed-book `sufficient` or `transfer` result for `verified-now`. A core-mechanism misconception blocks whole-paper completion regardless of aggregate score. Targeted reassessment covers only remediated nodes with a freshly frozen boundary case.

Award `retained` only for an independent later-session closed-book reconstruction at least seven full days after valid `verified-now` evidence.
