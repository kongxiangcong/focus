# Evidence-derived cognitive projection

The append-only evidence ledger is authority; `profile.yaml` is a rebuildable view and never drives routing. Preserve the ledger byte-for-byte during rebuild and derive identical output from identical valid evidence.

Evidence candidates identify type, conservative level candidate, dimensions, confidence, rubric statement, concept, verdict, and source artifact. Keep the learner answer in its interaction ledger rather than duplicating it. Corrections append superseding observed evidence; they never rewrite history.

Suggested UI levels are conservative: 0 no usable evidence or misconception; 1 sound recognition/definition; 2 purpose and coarse causal relation; 3 mechanism, assumption, and tradeoff; 4 transfer, alternative comparison, or evidence-based criticism. Self-report never raises a level. Durable node state remains separate: `provisional`, `verified-now`, and `retained` follow their explicit evidence gates.
