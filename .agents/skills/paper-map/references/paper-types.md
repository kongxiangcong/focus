# Paper-type mapping strategies

Choose one primary strategy and optional secondary traits from observable paper structure. Record this as a guide inference with confidence, never as an author claim.

- **Systems, architecture, compiler, ML systems:** trace objects across interfaces, mechanisms, resources, and evaluation. Connect choices to latency, throughput, capacity, energy, or programmability.
- **Algorithm/model:** map problem definition, objective, representation, steps, assumptions, complexity, and empirical support. Require reconstruction, objective-term explanation, ablation comparison, and transfer where appropriate.
- **Empirical/measurement:** map population, measurement pipeline, variables, controls, confounders, statistical claims, and external validity. Separate observation from causation.
- **Theory:** map definitions, assumptions, theorem dependencies, proof strategy, boundary cases, and counterexamples. Do not invent empirical evidence.
- **Survey/taxonomy/position:** map scope, selection method, organizing axes, synthesis claims, omissions, and the evidence-to-recommendation argument. A cited work is not independently verified merely because the paper mentions it.

Keep `author-claim`, `reported-evidence`, `guide-inference`, and `open-question` distinct. Use stable source anchors. Build dependency order around conceptual prerequisites rather than document order, and do not force formula, figure, or experiment nodes when the paper does not support them.
